The Rich History of FM Radio in Nepal

Nepal has one of the most vibrant and independent FM broadcasting ecosystems in South Asia. Ever since Radio Sagarmatha started broadcasting in 1997 as the first independent community radio in South Asia, local FM stations have been the primary source of music, local news, agriculture bulletins, and entertainment across the nation. Today, there are hundreds of licensed AM/FM transmitters scattered across the hills and plains of Nepal.

For Nepalis living abroad (in the Gulf, USA, Australia, Europe, or India), tuning into Nepali FM online is the ultimate way to stay connected to home. Through digital directories, these local broadcasts are accessible worldwide, bridging geographic distances instantly.

Top Nepali FM Online Stations to Stream Live

Here are the most popular Nepali radio stations that you can stream online for free:

  1. Hits FM 91.2: Broadcasting from Kathmandu, Hits FM is highly popular among youths for its English top 40, contemporary pop, and Nepali music count downs.
  2. Radio Kantipur: One of the most authoritative private commercial stations. Famous for news updates, call-in talk programs, and local pop hits.
  3. Image FM 97.9: A premier music station known for broadcasting classic rock, pop, and evergreen Nepali ghazals and songs.
  4. Radio Nepal: The national state broadcaster. Operating since 1951, it offers comprehensive national news, regional programs in multiple languages, and traditional folk music.
  5. Radio Sagarmatha 102.4: The pioneer community station broadcasting social issues, health programs, and local literature.
  6. Ujyaalo 90 Network: Famous for its rural news broadcast syndication "Kayakiran" and social welfare talk shows.

The Cultural Importance of Community Radio in Nepal

In a mountainous country where roads are difficult and telecommunication towers are sparse, radio remains the most reliable medium of communication. During natural disasters, radio networks are the sole source of safety bulletins.
